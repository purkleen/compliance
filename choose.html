How would you like to add risks?

Choose the way that suits you. All three lead to the same result: the risks you see, described in your own words and sent to compliance.

  • Ask me questions first

    Best ifYou are not sure where to start.

    Answer five short questions and we suggest the risks they point to. You confirm them and add detail.

    Start with questions

  • Select risks first

    Best ifYou already know which risks apply.

    Tick the risks you see on a list, with your own area at the top, then describe each one.

    Start with the list

  • I want to add risks manually

    Best ifYou have a specific risk in mind.

    Search for a risk or name your own and describe it, one at a time. You can also just upload files or leave a recording.

    Start adding risks

You can switch to another way at any time from the menu at the top. What you have entered in one does not carry over to another.
