Help us see the risks you see

Compliance is building one picture of the regulatory and ethical risks across the company. That picture is only as good as what people in each area tell us, so we are asking you to add what you notice in your daily work.

Why we are asking you

You see it firstProblems show up in daily work long before they reach a report. Nobody in compliance knows your area the way you do.
It shapes what gets fixedWhat you add goes into the company risk matrix, which is used to decide where controls, training and attention are needed.
It does not have to be polishedA few sentences, a document or a voice recording is enough. Compliance sorts it and comes back if something is unclear.
